How it works

Onboard it like a hire. Four steps, one afternoon.

STEP 01Give it a persona

A name, a personality, and a speaking style — pick a voice that sounds like your front desk should.

STEP 02Load the knowledge base

Your services, prices, hours, policies, and FAQs — everything it needs to answer like you would.

STEP 03Set call objectives

What each call should accomplish: qualify the caller, answer, book, take a deposit, or capture a message.

STEP 04Define escalation triggers

Exactly which topics, moods, or requests transfer to a human — instantly, mid-call.

AFTER EVERY CALL: FOLLOW-UP TEXT OR EMAIL SENT · CONTACT UPDATED · DEAL STAGE MOVED — AUTOMATICALLY
