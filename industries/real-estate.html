Will leads know they’re texting an AI first?
It’s your choice — many agents disclose “my assistant” and take over live the moment it’s warm. The handoff is seamless, and most leads won’t notice the difference in speed.
Can it qualify buyer vs. seller leads differently?
Yes — you configure separate flows for buyers, sellers, investors, and rental inquiries. Each gets a different qualification path and follow-up sequence tailored to their intent.
How does the long-term nurture work?
You set the cadence and content topics (market updates, rate alerts, local events). FYNZ drafts and sends — you approve or fully automate. The lead stays warm without any manual effort from you.
Does it integrate with my MLS or CRM?
FYNZ connects with popular tools via API and webhooks. Speak to our team about your specific stack and we’ll confirm compatibility during your demo.
