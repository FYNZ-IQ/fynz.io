Questions from restaurant owners

Does this replace my reservation platform?
It can take bookings directly, or capture guests alongside what you already use — your choice. FYNZ integrates with your existing setup or operates standalone.
Can guests opt out of messages?
Absolutely — one-word opt-out is always available, and frequency is fully in your control. Guests who opt in choose to hear from you, so engagement stays high.
How do QR codes work?
A guest scans, fills a 2-field form (name and phone), and joins your list instantly. FYNZ handles the capture and adds them automatically — no manual entry required.
How quickly can I send my first campaign?
Most restaurants send their first campaign within the first week of setup. Once your list has a few hundred guests, a slow-night offer can make a measurable difference the same evening.
Is this suitable for a single-location restaurant?
Yes — FYNZ works for single locations and multi-site groups alike. For single locations, the guest list and campaign tools are particularly impactful because every local guest matters.
