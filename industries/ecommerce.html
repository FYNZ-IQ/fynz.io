I already sell on another platform — does FYNZ still help?
Yes — the recovery flows, campaigns, and customer list work alongside your existing store. Integration with major platforms is supported, so you don't have to migrate to benefit from FYNZ automation.
How soon after abandonment does the first message send?
Configurable — most stores start with 1 hour for the first message and 24 hours for the follow-up with a discount. You set the timing and the tone; FYNZ handles the sending.
Can I A/B test different recovery messages?
Yes — test subject lines, send timing, and discount offers to find what works best for your customers. Results are tracked per variant so the winner is obvious.
