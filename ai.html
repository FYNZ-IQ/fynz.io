How FYNZ AI works

Hire once. Onboard in four steps.

Every agent shares the same setup model — fill it in once and the whole team knows your business.

STEP 01Give it a knowledge base

Your business info, services, prices, policies, and FAQs — the stuff you repeat all day.

STEP 02Set a persona

A name, a voice, and a tone that sounds like your business — friendly, formal, or somewhere between.

STEP 03Define objectives

What each interaction should accomplish: qualify, answer, book, collect, or follow up.

STEP 04Set escalation

Exactly when to hand to a human — by topic, sentiment, or a customer simply asking.
